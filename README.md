## Description

[View Project](https://mayamariaruth.github.io/science-museum/)

The **Oslo Science Museum** is a fun and interactive museum designed to spark curiosity in science for kids aged 7-15 and families with young children. The website aims to create excitement around the museum by offering a modern and engaging online experience that encourages visitors to come and explore the museum’s exhibits and events.

The design strikes a balance between appealing to a younger audience and maintaining a professional look. It's not overly childish but still fun and inviting. The goal is to make the website easy to navigate while showcasing the museum’s educational and innovative offerings, getting visitors excited to check it out in person.

### Key features

- A home page that introduces the museum with key info and eye-catching images.
- A contact page with the museum’s address, phone number, opening hours, and a simple contact form.
- A contribution page explaining how visitors can support the museum.
- An exhibitions page with details about current exhibits.
- An events page with details on upcoming events.
- Privacy policy and terms and conditions pages to ensure transparency.
- An information page with general details and additional resources for visitors.
- An about page providing details about the museum’s mission and history.
